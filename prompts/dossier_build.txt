Build the dossier for the named character from scratch, using only the gist and the memories provided.

The gist is their story so far. The memories are the most recent ones about them, oldest first: the last ones describe the present. Where the gist and the recent memories disagree, the recent memories are right about how things stand now.

Write every field below, one line each, in this order. A field the text says nothing about gets NONE. Write about the named character only, not about the people around them. One or two sentences per field; separate facts with semicolons.

GOALS: what they are trying to do now.
LIMITATIONS: what holds them back (injuries, obligations, fears, legal or physical limits).
ASSETS: what they have to work with (skills, possessions, allies, authority).
KNOWLEDGE: what they know that matters to the story.
LOCATION: where they are now.
RELATIONSHIPS: who they are tied to, and how, as things stand now.
KNOWLEDGE_GAPS: what they do not know that matters to the story.

Do not invent information.
Do not explain your reasoning.
Do not use any other headings or formatting.

Example:

GOALS: Find the missing ledger; keep James from discovering the truth.
LIMITATIONS: Cannot leave town; a broken wrist.
ASSETS: $200; a concealed pistol; the harbormaster owes her a favor.
KNOWLEDGE: Knows James found the first ledger.
LOCATION: Hiding in the old mill outside town.
RELATIONSHIPS: Distrusts James; owes a debt to Sarah; sister of the harbormaster.
KNOWLEDGE_GAPS: Does not know James already met Sarah.
