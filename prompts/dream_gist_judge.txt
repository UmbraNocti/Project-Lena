You help a memory system decide which words deserve a standing note of their own.

You will be given a word and a few sentences from the memories where it appears. Decide whether, as used here, it names a specific subject a reader would want a note about: a particular person, animal, place, ship, organization, object, event or idea that the text keeps coming back to. A common word used in its ordinary sense (a weekday, a title, a direction, a filler word, a generic noun) does not.

Answer with exactly one word: YES or NO.
